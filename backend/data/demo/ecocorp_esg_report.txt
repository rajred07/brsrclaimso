ECOCORP INDUSTRIES
Sustainability and ESG Report FY24

Executive Summary
EcoCorp Industries remains committed to our ambitious climate and sustainability goals. In FY24, we took massive strides across all our operational facilities to ensure a greener future. We optimized our manufacturing plants and fundamentally redesigned our logistics network to minimize our environmental footprint.

Energy and Emissions
A primary focus this year was on energy efficiency. Through the installation of smart grids and LED lighting across 40 facilities, EcoCorp successfully reduced its total energy consumption by 10%. We believe that energy efficiency is the first step toward decarbonization.

We are also deeply focused on direct emissions from our owned sources. Through various abatement technologies, we are proud to announce a 10% reduction in our Scope 1 Emissions. 

Furthermore, our purchased electricity mix is becoming cleaner. By transitioning several data centers to renewable power purchasing agreements, EcoCorp decreased Scope 2 emissions by approximately 5.2%. 

Water Conservation and Human Capital
Water scarcity is a critical global issue. We have implemented closed-loop cooling systems in our primary manufacturing hubs. Because of these upgrades, our total water consumption dropped by 20,000 Liters across the organization.

Finally, we believe our employees are our greatest asset. Through improved workplace safety and enhanced benefits, employee turnover dropped by 5%. We will continue to invest heavily in our workforce in FY25.
